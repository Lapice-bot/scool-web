Academic Policies

01

Attendance and participation

Students are required to attend at least 80% of their classes and participate in scheduled academic activities. Absences beyond the allowed limit may result in a Failed due to Absences (FA) mark. Students are also expected to be punctual and actively participate in school and co-curricular activities.

02

Academic Performance

Students are expected to complete all academic requirements and maintain satisfactory grades in their subjects. They should consistently participate in activities, examinations, and other assessments.

03

Proper Uniform

Students must wear the prescribed school uniform and follow the proper dress code during school hours. Students are expected to maintain a neat and appropriate appearance.

04

Academic Requirements

Students must submit assignments, projects, and other requirements within the given deadlines. Failure to complete required work may affect their grades and academic standing.
