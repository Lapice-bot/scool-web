Admission Requirements

For incoming learners

Junior High

Applicants must submit their report card, Certificate of Good Moral Character, conduct grade of 80% or equivalent, Baptismal and PSA Birth Certificates, and one 2x2 ID picture. Transferees must also submit Form 137-A or a certification of subjects taken.

Admission Procedure
1. Submit the requirements to the Registrar’s Office.
2. Complete the enrollment process.

For grade 11 applicants

Senior High

New Students and Transferees
New students and transferees must submit their original report card, Certificate of Good Moral Character, Baptismal and PSA Birth Certificates, and QVR/ESC Certificate if applicable. Advanced subjects require approval from the proper school officials.

Graduating Students
Grade 12 students must file their graduation application during the first term and complete all academic requirements set by DepEd to qualify for graduation.

For degree applicants

College

New Students/Freshmen
New students must submit their SHS Report Card or Form 138, PSA Birth Certificate, Certificate of Good Moral Character, and required photos and folders. Married students must also submit a Marriage Certificate.

Transferees
Transferees must submit their Honorable Dismissal or Transfer Credentials, PSA Birth Certificate, Certificate of Good Moral Character, and an informative copy of their Transcript of Records. They must also provide the required photos, folders, and envelopes, while married students must submit a Marriage Certificate.
