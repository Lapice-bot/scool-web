Student Life

Move, play, and belong

Sports

SMCM sports give learners a place to build discipline, teamwork, and confidence. Students can take part in basketball, volleyball, and badminton while learning to compete with respect and support one another.

Whether on the court or in practice, every game is an opportunity to grow through commitment and community.
