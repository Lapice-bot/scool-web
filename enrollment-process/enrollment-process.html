Enrollment process

01

Prepare your documents

Gather the required school records, identification documents, and completed forms for the level you are applying to.

02

Submit your application

Bring the documents to the admissions office or follow the current school instructions for submission and initial screening.

03

Complete assessment

Take any required assessment and attend the applicant interview or orientation scheduled by the school.

04

Confirm your enrollment

Review the enrollment details, settle the applicable school fees, and receive the next instructions for the opening of classes.
