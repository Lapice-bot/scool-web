Our vision & mission

Our vision and mission

St. Mary’s College of Meycauayan is a Catholic Ignacian-Marian institution that nurtures and empowers learners to become ethically and socially responsible individuals with global competence and lifelong skills. True to its Ignacian-Marian identity, the school aims to deepen discernment, strengthen faith, service, and excellence, promote innovative programs for global professionals, build resources and opportunities for a quality life, and develop the school in accordance with Catholic doctrine.
