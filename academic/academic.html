Academic

Foundation level

Junior High

SMCM offers young Marian learners basic skills and competencies in various subjects with Christian Living as the core subject, along with Mathematics, Science, Filipino, English, Araling Panlipunan, MAPEH, and HELE/TLE Computer.

The school also offers specialized courses that help Junior High School students develop practical skills and explore different areas of interest. These include Computer Systems Servicing, Cookery, Food and Beverage Services, Commercial Cooking, and Bread and Pastry Production, providing hands-on learning that prepares students for future studies and careers.

Pathways for the future

Senior High

STEM (Science, Technology, Engineering and Mathematics)

Prepares the students to acquire knowledge and interpret scientific processes of the natural world and generate and evaluate scientific facts and evidence to become critical thinkers as they contribute to the development of new scientific knowledge.

ABM (Accountancy, Business and Management)

Prepares the students to become efficient entrepreneurs equipped with knowledge and skills in management consultancy, business organizations, and deepened financial literacy.

HUMSS (Humanities and Social Science)

Prepares the students to become equipped with 21st century skills needed to hone their potentials in analyzing substantial content of philosophical, literary, aesthetic, and religious perspectives applicable to social sciences professional careers.

College programs

College

BSIT (Bachelor of Science in Information Technology)

Christian values and principles, psychological, technological preparations, which will form foundation of good professional procedures and practices to answer the challenges of a globally competitive business and technical community towards a self-reliant national economy.

BSBA (Bachelor of Science in Business Administration)

Christian values and principles, business knowledge, leadership skills, and practical preparation to develop responsible and competent professionals who can meet the challenges of a globally competitive business environment and contribute to a productive and self-reliant economy.

BSED (Bachelor of Secondary Education)

Professional competencies, ethics, social awareness involvement, responsibility and keen sense of Filipino culture that will enhance the true pursuit of knowledge through relevant teaching methods and classroom management.
